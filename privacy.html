SpotBox

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

SpotBox is a personal atlas app. This policy describes what data the app collects, where it lives, and how you can remove it. Plain language, no dark corners.

Who runs SpotBox

SpotBox is an independent app operated by Tushar Mittal. For any privacy question, email tusharmittal645@gmail.com.

What data we collect

Account information

  • Anonymous account — an anonymous ID is created when you first open the app so your data has somewhere to live. No email, no name.
  • Google Sign-In (optional) — if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email, display name, and profile picture URL. This is used only to identify you across your own devices and to display your profile inside the app.

Content you save

  • Places you save (name, address, coordinates, category, tags, notes, cover photo).
  • Collections and trips you create.
  • Photos you pick as cover images (stored on your device only, in the app's private cache).
  • Journal entries you write against a trip or a place.

Device access

  • Approximate/precise location — used only to center the map on your position and enable the "near me" filter. Your location is not written to our servers.
  • Photos — the app uses the system photo picker; only photos you explicitly pick are read. Selected photos are compressed and stored on your device.
  • Internet — used to sync your saved places to Firestore and to talk to Google Maps and Google Places.

What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics. No crash reporting SDKs. No advertising IDs.
  • No contacts, no phone number, no calendar, no microphone, no camera direct capture.
  • No third-party trackers. No behavioural profiling.

Where your data is stored

  • Google Firebase (Firestore + Firebase Authentication) — your saved places, trips, collections and profile data are stored in a Firebase project operated by us. Firebase servers are run by Google. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Your device — cover photos and thumbnails are cached locally in the app's private storage.

Who can see your data

Only you. Access to your Firestore documents is enforced by Firebase security rules that check request.auth.uid == uid for every read and write. There is no social feed, no sharing, no public listing. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Third-party services SpotBox talks to

  • Google Firebase — authentication and data storage. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Sign-In — used only if you choose to link a Google account. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps SDK — renders the map inside the app. Google may log map tile requests per their policies.
  • Google Places API — used when you search for a place. The text you type in the search box is sent to Google to return suggestions.

These services are covered by Google's own privacy policies. We do not send them any data beyond what is strictly needed for the feature to work.

How long we keep your data

Your data lives in your account as long as the account exists. If you delete the account (see below), everything is removed within a few seconds.

Deleting your data

Open SpotBox → Profile → scroll to Danger Zone → Delete account. This cascades:

  • All places, collections, trips, journal entries and tags you saved.
  • Your Firebase Auth user record.
  • Every locally cached photo and thumbnail.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you cannot open the app but still want your data removed, email tusharmittal645@gmail.com from the address linked to your Google account and we will delete it manually.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR / CCPA / similar), you may have the right to access, correct, port, or delete your personal data. SpotBox already provides all of this in-app: your data is shown to you in the UI, editable inline, and one button deletes it. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

SpotBox is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update the "Last updated" date at the top when this policy changes. Material changes will be surfaced inside the app before they take effect.

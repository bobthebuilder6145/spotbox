Delete your account

Two ways to remove your data. Both wipe everything.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open SpotBox on your phone.
  2. Tap the Profile tab (bottom-right).
  3. Scroll to the bottom → Danger Zone.
  4. Tap Delete account → confirm.

This immediately removes everything and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it for you

If you can't open the app or would rather we do it, email us from the address linked to your Google account and we'll delete your account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Every place, collection, trip, journal entry and tag you saved.
  • Your Firebase Auth user record (email, name, profile picture URL, uid).
  • Every cover photo and thumbnail cached on your device.

Nothing is retained. There is no backup, no recovery period, no separate archive. Once deleted, the data is gone.

Non-personal data

SpotBox does not collect analytics, crash reports, or advertising IDs, so there is nothing else to purge on our side.
